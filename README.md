面向需要保留原表演与镜头结构的创作者，用于替换视频中的人物、背景、产品或道具。用户提供源视频，并按需补充每个替换目标对应的参考图或参考视频；流程先分析镜头、动作、遮挡、接触、节奏、声音与素材适配度，再确认替换范围与必须保留的内容。每一份参考素材都会绑定明确职责，替换后检查身份一致性、几何结构、跨镜头连续性和音画同步。最终交付遵循原动作与摄影结构的替换成片。不处理字幕、普通剪辑、调色、转码或静态图动画化。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入源视频与参考素材 → 输出延续原动作与摄影结构的替换成片
- 逐镜头分析动作、遮挡、接触、节奏与声音，先划清哪些能换、哪些必须留
- 为每个替换目标绑定专属参考素材职责，避免素材张冠李戴
- 覆盖人物、背景、产品、道具以及同时替换的多个可见元素
- 交付前检查身份一致性、几何结构、跨镜头连续性与音画同步
- 明确不接字幕清理、常规剪辑、调色、转码与静态图动画

## 🇬🇧 English

For creators who need semantic element replacement without losing the source performance. You supply a source video plus optional image or video references for each target, and the workflow inspects shots, motion, occlusion, contact, timing, audio, and reference suitability. It then confirms what changes and what must remain, binds every reference to a clear role, and picks an audio strategy before generating. The result follows the original action and camera structure and is checked for identity, geometry, continuity, and synchronization. It is not for subtitles, routine editing, color grading, transcoding, or still-image animation.

### ✨ What it does

- Source video plus references in → replacement video that follows the original action and camera structure
- Shot-by-shot review of motion, occlusion, contact, timing, and audio before anything changes
- Binds each reference image or clip to one clear target role
- Covers people, backgrounds, products, props, or several visible elements at once
- Checks identity, geometry, cross-shot continuity, and audio sync before delivery
- Out of scope: subtitles, routine editing, color grading, transcoding, still-image animation
